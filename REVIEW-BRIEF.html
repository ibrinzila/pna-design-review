PNA: from reading a plan to acting on evidence

Advisory design brief, 15 September 2026. Prepared for Claude Design and other independent reviewers. This is an assessment brief, not an approved redesign or a claim of product readiness.

Mission and intended outcome

Help people understand Moldova's EU-accession commitments, inspect the evidence behind assessments, contribute or challenge evidence, follow a reasoned response, and see what changes. The user should leave better able to act, not merely better informed. A readable catalogue is a foundation; the intended product also needs a complete evidence and accountability loop.

The owner welcomes a substantial redesign, a focused evidence overlay, or another well-argued direction. The current green pna/eu identity is reference material, not a constraint that should defeat the intended outcome. Romanian-first access, accessible mobile use, source fidelity, version history and distinct review authority remain important constraints. Do not imitate a comparator's dashboard without reconstructing its measurement model.

Current truth and its boundaries

The application baseline inspected on 15 September was public release 4, source revision 216aa1b5ce42e5b13d1e3d998c17ba1c4e0402d6. Publishing this separate review packet does not complete the product capabilities described here. The product has 2,779 indexed action records, 105 source documents, 24 AI-reviewed public explanations and a private 53-record editorial pilot. The live editorial event table was empty during this audit. No human calibration or legal-source certification is inferred from the deployed interface.

Public source records retain planned indicators, institutions, dates, original PDF pages, field passages and table coordinates. Public contributions have a private receipt and an editorial decision path. The separate pilot supports claim decisions, corrections and revision-bound history. Every action's implementation field currently says not-assessed. There is no implemented benchmark registry, observed indicator series, assessment calculation, topic-follow subscription, authority-response workflow or change-to-score propagation.

The restricted workflow can be assessed through the separate demonstration and frozen screens. Demonstration decisions are synthetic and have no production effect. They are not evidence of an actual person's review. The pack distinguishes live observations, mirrored current UI, scripted states and proposed behavior.

The argument to assess

People cannot exercise the intended agency if the displayed assessment, its source, and the route to affect it are separate destinations. A plausible product model is therefore one evidence-linked case that supports progressively deeper tasks: orient, inspect, compare, contribute, adjudicate and revisit. This does not yet establish whether a drawer, canvas, split view or separate workspace is best. Those are alternatives to test against the same journeys.

An assessment needs a typed subject and measure, an observation period, an attributed assessor, a rubric or calculation, the supporting and conflicting evidence, and a version. An original document proves what its author said; it does not by itself prove real-world implementation or endorse PNA's interpretation. An EC readiness category, annual progress judgement, PNA delivery milestone, quantitative output, legal adoption event and social outcome must not share an unexplained progress bar.

Design-review request

First reconstruct the mission, constraints, current capabilities and missing product model in your own words. Then identify blocking/substantive/minor findings with an evidence pointer, confidence and what would change your mind. Do not start with a new home-page visual.

Produce three genuinely different directions: an extension of current pages; a case-centred evidence workspace; and a more substantial organisation of the product. Recommend one, including migration effort, ongoing editorial obligation and what can remain unchanged. Keep the same evidence, privacy and authority rules in every alternative.

Demonstrate the same critical journeys in each direction. Include information architecture, source preview with surrounding context, mobile transitions, contribution and response states, disagreement, missing evidence, stale corrections, changed source, and changes affecting an aggregate. Describe what backend meaning each visible status requires. Mark any simulated data conspicuously.

Acceptance questions for proposals

  • Can someone reveal the exact source passage and the scoring/interpretation rule in at most two deliberate actions from a displayed assessment? This is a proposed interaction target, not a proven usability result.
  • Can they distinguish a target, a reported observation, an independent assessment and an unresolved dispute without reading a methodology essay?
  • Can they correct a specific claim or mapping, see its review status and recover their work?
  • Can several organisations disagree transparently without one unlabelled number replacing all views?
  • Can a mobile reader inspect evidence and return to the claim with their place preserved?
  • Can an editor tell which current outputs are affected when evidence or an accepted interpretation changes?
  • Does the design reduce time to a defensible answer and correction effort without increasing unsupported acceptance?

Evaluate with representative task observations, error detection and correction outcomes. Page views, dwell time, citation clicks, AI agreement and number of completed forms do not prove understanding, independence or civic influence.

Scope of this pack

The pack documents the current site, not every individual page among thousands of repeated records. Its screen manifest covers route families and material interaction states; explicit gaps remain visible. Source snippets are a versioned design reference, not a complete production database. The live private workspace and its access controls remain separate. Research from Evidence ETL informs the separation of source, candidate and human judgement; no Promo-LEX private corpus, tender material or operational data is included.

Frozen evidence and a synthetic demonstration. Independent civic initiative; no government endorsement. Scope & reproducibility
