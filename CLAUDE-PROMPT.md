Assess PNA.md as a civic evidence and accountability product, then propose genuinely different design directions. Do not begin by making the home page prettier.

Read REVIEW-BRIEF.md and DESIGN-REVIEW.md, inspect the live links and frozen screen-manifest.json, use the isolated demonstration, and inspect source-manifest.json plus the selected current UI/server contracts. RESEARCH.md compares IPRE's two trackers and relevant international patterns. Report any asset or workflow you cannot access; do not infer it from a screenshot.

Our mission: people should learn what Moldova has committed to, inspect the precise evidence and method behind an assessment, correct or contribute evidence, receive an accountable response, and follow what changes. Citizens, researchers/journalists, shadow-report teams, government partners, subject specialists and returning followers have different jobs and authority. These are provisional roles; do not invent completed user research.

Current truth: 2,779 indexed records and 105 documents; 24 public AI-reviewed explanations; a private 53-record review pilot; no human calibration established. Implementation is unassessed for all indexed records. The catalogue, evidence passages, private contribution receipts, moderation, source editions and correction history exist. Typed implementation observations, benchmark mappings, assessment calculations, organisational responses, subscriptions and propagation of corrected evidence to scores do not. Demonstration decisions are synthetic; never treat them as public implementation evidence or genuine human acceptance.

Your first deliverable is your reconstruction of the problem and ranked findings with evidence, confidence and what would change your mind. Then compare: (1) extending current pages, (2) a case-centred evidence workspace, (3) a fuller reorganisation. Recommend one based on user outcomes, migration and editorial workload. A radical redesign is welcome if justified; an overlay is equally welcome if it solves the problem.

Use the same cases in each direction: a citizen checking the youth-centres annual target; a researcher tracing an illustrative readiness score to the EC passage and conversion rule; a source disagreement; a citizen contribution and receipt; an editor correction and stale-work recovery; an authority response; and a follower returning after a correction. Label proposed journeys and simulated observations. Readiness level, annual progress, recommendation fulfilment, programme delivery, legal adoption and social outcomes are not interchangeable metrics.

Show desktop and mobile task paths, first viewport, source-preview context and return behavior, keyboard/focus handling, empty/loading/error states, insufficient evidence, competing assessments, revision history and role transitions. Explain the data/authority meaning required by each component. Preserve public reading, source and revision identity, privacy, independent review and explicit unknowns. Do not remove safeguards solely to make a prototype flow smoothly.

Aim for precise evidence plus its method in at most two deliberate actions from a displayed assessment; this is a proposed target to test. Measure time to a defensible answer, misinterpretation, critical-error detection, correction effort, contribution disposition and follow-up understanding. Click counts and time-on-page alone do not prove comprehension or agency.

Return a concise product argument, a role/job map, an information architecture, a component/state model, three comparable directions, a recommended increment with trade-offs, and a validation plan. List dependencies and decisions requiring the owner. Do not imply a submitted suggestion changes an official result or grants publication authority. Keep observations, interpretation and recommendations separate.
